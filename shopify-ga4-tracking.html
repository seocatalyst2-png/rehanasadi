Frequently Asked Questions

What is Shopify GA4 tracking? +
Shopify GA4 tracking is the specialized implementation of Google Analytics 4 for Shopify stores. It captures the entire customer journey—from product impressions and cart updates to checkout steps and confirmed purchases—with structured ecommerce parameters and verified transaction data.
Which events do you set up? +
I configure standard GA4 ecommerce events supported by your store architecture, including view_item_list, select_item, view_item, add_to_cart, remove_from_cart, view_cart, begin_checkout, add_shipping_info, add_payment_info, and purchase.
Do you track purchase value correctly? +
Yes. Every purchase event is configured to transmit the exact order ID, currency code, purchase revenue, tax, shipping, and item-scoped details. This ensures GA4 monetization reports align directly with Shopify's actual order totals.
Do I still need the default Shopify tracking? +
Yes. Shopify's native analytics and GA4 serve distinct, complementary purposes. Shopify provides built-in sales, customer, and inventory dashboards, while GA4 enables deep cross-channel attribution and user journey exploration. The implementation avoids duplicate tags while preserving Shopify's built-in metrics.
Can you fix my existing setup? +
Yes. Many stores suffer from tracking breaks caused by Shopify's checkout extensibility update, theme changes, or duplicate app tags. I audit your store, identify why events or revenue are missing, and resolve the underlying configuration issues.
Will this work with Meta and Google Ads? +
Yes. While GA4, Meta, and Google Ads use separate conversion tags, a clean ecommerce measurement setup provides structured data that supports all downstream advertising pixels. For advanced setups, I also implement Meta Conversions API (CAPI) and Google Ads enhanced conversions.
How long does setup take? +
Setup duration depends on your current Shopify store plan, theme architecture, custom event requirements, third-party apps, and existing issues. The timeline is confirmed after an initial technical inspection of your store.
Do you set up enhanced ecommerce? +
Yes. GA4 ecommerce tracking includes item-scoped parameters such as item ID, item name, price, quantity, brand, and item category. This allows you to evaluate product performance, cart abandonment, and conversion rates across different merchandise lines.
Do you use server-side tracking? +
When required, yes. For merchants who want greater data control, reduced reliance on browser tags, and higher event match quality, I set up Server-Side Tracking using server-side Google Tag Manager and Stape.
Can you set up custom events? +
Yes. If your store has custom interactive elements—such as bundle builders, drawer cart interactions, size guides, newsletter popups, or currency selectors—I configure dedicated custom events and parameters tailored to your store.
Do you support multiple stores? +
Yes. If your business operates multiple regional or localized Shopify stores (e.g. US, UK, EU stores), I can configure multi-store tracking architecture with dedicated or roll-up GA4 data streams to maintain clean regional reporting.
Do you work with Shopify Plus? +
Yes. I implement tracking for both standard Shopify stores (using Custom Web Pixels and Customer Events API) and Shopify Plus stores (including checkout extensibility customizations and dedicated checkout branding).
Is there a fixed price? +
Because Shopify store architectures, theme implementations, custom event requirements, and marketing integrations vary in scope, pricing is customized to your exact requirements. Reach out via WhatsApp with your store details for a transparent quote.
What information do you need to get started? +
To begin, I will need collaborator access to your Shopify store admin, access to your Google Tag Manager container (if using GTM), and edit access to your GA4 property, along with notes on any specific tracking discrepancies you have identified.
