Common questions

Do I need server-side tracking if I already have GA4?

Not always. If paid ads, iOS traffic, or ad-blocked audiences are important, server-side tracking often recovers meaningful conversion data.

What access do you need to get started?

Usually GTM editor, GA4 editor, ad platform access where relevant, and platform admin access (Shopify, WooCommerce, or custom CMS).

Can you work with my existing agency or developer?

Yes. I provide practical technical specs and coordinate implementation while keeping your team aligned.

Do you support platforms beyond Shopify?

Yes, including WooCommerce, Magento, custom stores, SaaS apps, and lead-generation sites.

What happens after project delivery?

One-time projects include a short support window. Ongoing retainer is available for continuous monitoring and updates.

Are prices fixed?

Listed prices are starting points for standard scope. Final pricing depends on platform complexity and integration depth.
