Service 02

Server-Side Tracking (sGTM)

Browser-based tracking is increasingly degraded by Safari ITP cookie caps, ad-blocking extensions, and iOS privacy updates. Server-Side Google Tag Manager moves data collection from the visitor's browser to your own first-party cloud server, extending cookie life and recovering lost conversion signals.

What's Included in This Service
  • ✓ Dedicated sGTM container provisioning on Stape.io or Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
  • ✓ Custom first-party subdomain setup (e.g., data.yourbrand.com) with SSL and DNS routing
  • ✓ First-party cookie persistence engineering to bypass Safari ITP 7-day expirations (up to 400 days)
  • ✓ Client-side tag migration to sGTM: routing GA4, Meta CAPI, and Google Ads through your server
  • ✓ Header stripping and PII protection to maintain GDPR/ePrivacy compliance

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about services & delivery.

What tracking platforms do you work with? +
I specialize in Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Tag Manager (Web & Server-Side containers), Meta Conversions API (CAPI), Google Ads conversion tracking, and Stape.io. On the store side, I work with Shopify, Shopify Plus, WooCommerce, Magento, custom web apps, and CMS platforms.
Can you fix an existing GA4 or GTM setup without starting over? +
Yes. Many engagements involve auditing and refining existing containers. I clean up orphaned tags, resolve duplicate purchase firing triggers, align measurement IDs, and ensure proper dataLayer variables without disrupting existing historical reporting.
Do I need server-side tracking if I already have standard GA4 and Meta Pixel? +
If your business spends meaningfully on paid advertising or receives substantial traffic from iOS / Safari devices, server-side tracking is highly recommended. Client-side pixels lose 20% to 40% of conversion data due to ad-blockers and Safari 7-day ITP caps. Server-side tagging (sGTM) recovers this lost data and extends cookie persistence up to 400 days.
What access is required to begin implementation? +
Typically, I require Editor access to Google Tag Manager, Administrator/Editor access to GA4, Partner or Admin access to Meta Business Manager / Events Manager, and standard collaborator or staff access to your website CMS (e.g., Shopify Collaborator or WordPress Administrator).
Can you coordinate with our existing web developer or marketing agency? +
Yes. I frequently collaborate with internal development teams, agency account managers, and freelance media buyers. I provide precise technical dataLayer specifications and handle all GTM/server configurations directly, minimizing the workload for your engineers.
How do you validate conversion accuracy before project completion? +
I conduct end-to-end sandbox and live testing. Every conversion action is validated in GTM Preview mode, GA4 DebugView, and Meta Test Events. Following live release, I reconcile incoming analytics purchases directly against your store backend order numbers to confirm 100% parity.
Do I need to start with a diagnostic tracking audit? +
If you have a clear, specific requirement (such as setting up a new server container or adding Meta CAPI), we can proceed directly to implementation. If you are experiencing unexplained discrepancies or inherited a broken setup, the diagnostic tracking audit is the recommended starting point to identify root causes and map out fixes.
How are project scopes and quotes determined? +
Every tracking project is scoped based on your specific tech stack, domain architecture, and measurement objectives. Single-domain setups with standard platforms are scoped quickly, while complex multi-domain environments or custom headless stores receive a clear, transparent quote before any work begins.
