Frequently Asked Questions

What does the audit include? +
The audit comprehensively reviews your GA4 configuration, Google Tag Manager tags and triggers, data layer schemas, ad platform pixels (Meta CAPI, Google Ads), server-side containers, and ecommerce checkout events. You receive an itemized diagnostic report with prioritized technical fixes.
How long does it take? +
Turnaround depends on the scope and complexity of your tracking setup, number of platforms, and data layer architecture. Once container access is provided, audits are reviewed and delivered promptly.
Do you also implement the fixes? +
Yes. Beyond delivering actionable audit documentation, I provide direct hands-on implementation and technical remediation across GA4, GTM, Meta CAPI, and server-side containers.
Is there a fixed price for the audit? +
Audit scope varies based on your platform stack, store architecture (Shopify or custom eCommerce setups), and whether server-side containers are involved. Reach out via WhatsApp with your store details to receive an upfront scope and quote.
Which platforms do you audit? +
I audit tracking architectures across Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager (web & server), Meta Pixel & Conversions API (CAPI), Google Ads Conversion Tracking, Shopify, and custom eCommerce web applications.
Who is the audit for? +
The audit is designed for eCommerce store owners, marketing managers, and performance media buyers scaling paid ad spend who need 100% confidence in their conversion data, attribution, and revenue reporting.
