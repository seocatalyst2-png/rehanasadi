E-COMMERCE Netherlands

Slageronline.nl

Dutch online butcher & gourmet meat delivery

GA4 Server-Side (sGTM) Meta CAPI Stape.io Looker Studio

Client was running Meta Ads with a browser-only pixel. Approximately 35% of purchase events were lost to Safari ITP and Dutch ad blockers, skewing attribution and starving ad algorithms of critical conversion signals.

✓ +22%
Event Match Quality (EMQ) lift on Meta CAPI
✓ ~35%
Missing purchase conversions recovered
✓ 1st-Party
sGTM container with deduplication logic
Get Quote →

The Problem

The client ran Meta Ads (~€100/day) relying entirely on standard browser tracking. Due to strict iOS Safari ITP caps and prevalent privacy extensions across the Netherlands, roughly 35% of orders never reached Meta's Events Manager. Ad delivery algorithms optimized on distorted conversion signals, inflating apparent acquisition costs.

What I Built

  1. Deployed a server-side GTM container via Stape.io on a custom first-party subdomain (sgtm.slageronline.nl).
  2. Engineered Meta Conversions API (CAPI) with full event deduplication using matched event_id parameters.
  3. Mapped full eCommerce event schema: view_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout, and purchase.
  4. Connected enhanced GA4 eCommerce measurement to automated weekly Looker Studio dashboards.

Business Impact

Event Match Quality (EMQ) jumped by +22%, bypassing ad-blocker drop-offs and restoring true attribution. With clean purchase values feeding Meta's smart bidding algorithms, campaign ROAS stabilized, enabling confident ad spend scaling.

DTC E-COMMERCE Netherlands & Nordics

NordSleeps

Scandinavian DTC premium mattress & sleep brand

GA4 Rebuild Server-Side (sGTM) Shopify Stape.io ITP Resilience

High-AOV sleep products have extended consideration cycles. Safari ITP capped tracking cookies at 7 days, severing return-visitor attribution. Meanwhile, 40+ orphaned legacy tags in GTM caused severe event inflation and duplicate purchases.

✓ 400 Days
Up to 400-day first-party cookie persistence in the documented setup
✓ 40+ Tags
Orphaned tags purged in full GTM consolidation
✓ Zero Dupes
Duplicate purchase triggers were removed and validated
Get Quote →

The Problem

NordSleeps mattresses involve high-consideration purchases with buyers taking 14 to 30 days to purchase. Safari's 7-day cookie limit affected returning user attribution across long consideration cycles. In addition, an inherited GTM container with 40+ orphaned agency tags caused duplicate event triggers.

What I Built

  1. Audited the entire container, deleted 40+ obsolete tags, and implemented strict tag-firing governance.
  2. Deployed server-side GTM on Stape.io with Set-Cookie HTTP headers, establishing up to 400-day first-party cookie persistence in the documented setup.
  3. Re-architected GA4 eCommerce tracking with item-scoped parameters for the Shopify checkout funnel.
  4. Relocated client-side marketing tags into the server container to improve page speed and ensure duplicate purchase triggers were removed and validated.

Business Impact

First-touch discovery attribution was fully restored across 30-day buying cycles. Duplicate orders dropped to zero, providing the leadership team with dependable acquisition metrics for their multi-market scaling campaigns.

B2B E-COMMERCE Netherlands

Packaging Discounter

B2B industrial packaging & shipping supplies

GA4 Audit Shopify Web Pixels Google Ads Enhanced Conversions DebugView QA

GA4 was reporting roughly 60% fewer conversions than actual Shopify orders. Conflicting purchase scripts across theme liquid and third-party apps were misfiring, while Google Ads Smart Bidding was operating on incomplete revenue data.

✓ 100%
Exact order reconciliation between Shopify and GA4
✓ 3 Tags
Conflicting purchase implementations unified
✓ +60%
Conversion data recovered for Google Ads bidding
Get Quote →

The Problem

Packaging Discounter's GA4 reports showed a 60% deficit against actual Shopify back-office sales. Three overlapping purchase scripts fired on conflicting thank-you page triggers, while staging measurement IDs contaminated production analytics streams.

What I Built

  1. Conducted a forensic tracking audit, identifying 3 conflicting purchase tags and legacy script injections.
  2. Rebuilt GTM container from scratch with a single authoritative purchase trigger utilizing Shopify Web Pixels API.
  3. Corrected GA4 measurement ID routing and verified end-to-end data integrity in GA4 DebugView.
  4. Implemented Google Ads Enhanced Conversions with customer parameter hashing for automated bidding accuracy.

Business Impact

Delivered 100% order reconciliation between Shopify backend sales and GA4 analytics. Google Ads Smart Bidding regained full visibility into real order values, eliminating bidding blindness and stabilizing B2B acquisition costs.
